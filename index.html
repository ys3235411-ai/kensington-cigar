Our Story

A Legacy of Distinction,
Rooted in Kensington

Founded in 1987 by a Cuban émigré with an uncompromising love for the leaf, our shop has been a quiet institution on Kensington Church Street for nearly four decades.

We are not a catalogue. We are a conversation — between grower and smoker, between tradition and the pleasure of discovery. Every visit is an education.
